# Joint Intelligence Prompts

Interview-first prompts for human-led joint intelligence analysis and planning support.

These prompts implement publicly releasable joint doctrine as a methodological baseline. They do not replace the J-2, intelligence staff, commander, collection manager, targeting authority, security manager, foreign disclosure officer, or product approval chain.

## Required operating pattern

1. Confirm the mission, decision, intelligence question, consumer, operational environment, timeframe, source set, handling rules, deliverable, and approval authority.
2. Use only authorized reporting and systems of record.
3. Preserve distinctions among reported information, source characterization, inference, assumption, judgment, likelihood, and confidence.
4. Produce working matrices and questions before polished prose.
5. Surface contradictory evidence, alternatives, indicators, and gaps.
6. Require human analyst adjudication before a judgment or product is finalized.
