# Ownership and Finance Mapping

## Purpose
Reconstruct ownership, control, financing, and operational relationships for any entity, asset, program, transaction, or project.

## Interaction rule
Ask the user to supply or confirm the target, jurisdictions, relevant dates, materiality threshold, evidence corpus, desired depth, and definitions of ownership and control. Clarify whether the task concerns legal ownership, beneficial ownership, practical control, financial exposure, or all of them.

## Required parameters
- Target entity, asset, project, or transaction
- Jurisdictions and time period
- Approved evidence sources
- Materiality or ownership threshold
- Research purpose and audience

## Prompt
Using only the approved records, map the ownership, financing, governance, and operating chain for the target.

Distinguish where applicable:
- Legal owner
- Ultimate beneficial owner
- Parent, subsidiary, affiliate, and special-purpose vehicle
- Shareholder and voting-right holder
- Board or management control
- Creditor, lender, guarantor, insurer, and collateral holder
- Grantor, concession holder, operator, maintainer, and vendor
- Data controller, administrator, and hosting provider
- Government, institutional, or private sponsor

For each relationship provide the exact source, date, jurisdiction, percentage or right involved, confidence, and unresolved ambiguity. Separate formal legal rights from inferred practical influence. Identify shell entities, circular ownership, nominee arrangements, related-party transactions, refinancing, covenants, step-in rights, veto rights, exclusivity, and termination rights when evidenced.

Do not infer control from nationality, affiliation, funding, or association alone. End with unresolved entities, contradictory records, and the specific documents needed to close each gap.
