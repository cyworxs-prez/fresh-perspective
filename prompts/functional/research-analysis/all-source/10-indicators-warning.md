# Indicators and Warning

## Purpose
Develop observable, diagnostic, and decision-linked indicators for any forecasted event, condition, risk, opportunity, or strategic change.

## Interaction rule
Ask for missing critical parameters: forecasted outcome, relevant actors, baseline, geographic or organizational scope, warning horizon, collection capabilities, decision thresholds, and actions available to the user. Do not design indicators until the outcome and supported decision are clear.

## Required parameters
- Forecasted outcome or condition
- Supported decision and decision-maker
- Scope and warning horizon
- Baseline and known sequence
- Available collection sources
- Required lead time and decision thresholds

## Prompt
Develop indicators across the domains relevant to the approved problem, such as political, diplomatic, informational, military or security, economic, financial, technical, cyber, intelligence, legal, infrastructure, social, environmental, organizational, coalition, and temporal activity.

For every indicator specify:
- Indicator statement
- Actor, node, action, or condition observed
- Current baseline
- Observable deviation or threshold
- Expected sequence and timing
- Estimated lead time
- Collection source and cadence
- Reliability and manipulability
- Alternative explanations
- Diagnostic value across competing hypotheses
- Decision, collection, or mitigation action triggered
- Reset or expiration condition

Separate leading, coincident, and lagging indicators. Distinguish single observations from patterns and cumulative thresholds. Prioritize indicators that are observable in time, specific, diagnostic, decision-linked, and difficult to fake. Identify blind spots and collection dependencies.
