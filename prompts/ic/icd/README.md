# Intelligence Community Directive Prompts

Interview-first prompts that help human analysts and reviewers apply publicly releasable Intelligence Community Directives and related implementation guidance.

These prompts provide diagnostic assistance. They do not certify organizational compliance, determine classification, approve dissemination, replace legal or policy interpretation, or represent nonpublic element-specific procedures.

## Operating pattern

1. Confirm that the directive applies to the product and organization.
2. Identify the current directive, implementation standard, and local product-line guidance.
3. Interview the analyst about the intelligence question, consumer, product type, evidence base, information cutoff, handling restrictions, and review stage.
4. Review the actual evidence, sourcing, and reasoning rather than polished prose alone.
5. Separate directive findings, local requirements, and recommended tradecraft.
6. Present findings for analyst and reviewer adjudication rather than silently rewriting.
