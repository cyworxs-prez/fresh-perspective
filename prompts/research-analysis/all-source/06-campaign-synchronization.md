# Campaign Synchronization Analysis

## Purpose
Determine whether multiple activities are independent, coincidental, mutually reinforcing, or components of a coordinated campaign.

## Interaction rule
Ask the user to clarify the actor set, activity or event corpus, candidate objective, geography, time period, decision relevance, and evidence boundary. If no candidate objective is supplied, ask whether to derive candidate objectives from the evidence or analyze coordination without an intent judgment.

## Required parameters
- Event or activity dataset
- Relevant actors and stakeholders
- Geographic and temporal scope
- Supported question or decision
- Approved evidence sources
- Coordination standard or threshold, if established

## Prompt
Code each supplied event by:
- Date and location
- Actor, intermediary, target, and beneficiary
- Action and mechanism or instrument
- Resource committed
- Communication or narrative
- Observed result
- Source and confidence

Then test for:
- Common or compatible objectives
- Sequencing and preparatory activity
- Mutual reinforcement across mechanisms
- Shared actors, intermediaries, infrastructure, resources, or narratives
- Central direction, delegated coordination, informal alignment, or opportunistic convergence
- Activation of latent relationships during a decision, dispute, crisis, or opportunity
- Adaptation after resistance, exposure, or failure
- Evidence of deconfliction, command, tasking, funding, or feedback

Produce:
1. Event table
2. Candidate lines of effort or campaign hypotheses
3. Evidence supporting coordination
4. Evidence against coordination
5. Independent-action and coincidence explanations
6. Gaps and diagnostic collection needs
7. Confidence assessment

Do not equate temporal proximity, shared interests, similar messaging, or common identity with coordination.
