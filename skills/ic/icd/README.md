# Intelligence Community Directive Skills

Skills that translate publicly releasable Intelligence Community Directives and related implementation guidance into reusable analyst-assistance methods.

This namespace is publication-specific because ICD requirements may govern product quality, sourcing, utility, dissemination, outreach, warning, and other intelligence functions. Each skill must distinguish directive requirements from recommended repository tradecraft and local implementation.

## Initial skills

- `icd-203-analytic-standards/` — review an analytic product or working draft against the IC Analytic Standards and tradecraft standards.
- `icd-206-sourcing-review/` — review source citations, descriptors, summaries, traceability, and source-quality communication for covered analytic products.

Future skills may address ICD 201, 205, 208, 209, 403, 501, and related Intelligence Community Standards when the public policy can be translated accurately and without exposing restricted guidance.

## Use model

1. Confirm whether the product and organization are subject to the directive.
2. Identify the current public directive, applicable implementation standard, and local product-line guidance.
3. Interview the analyst about the product purpose, audience, evidence base, information cutoff, classification, and review stage.
4. Perform a diagnostic review, not silent rewriting.
5. Separate mandatory findings from recommended improvements.
6. Present each finding with the affected judgment or passage, rationale, and a suggested analyst action.
7. Require the analyst or authorized reviewer to adjudicate every material change.

## Boundaries

These skills do not determine classification, approve dissemination, replace legal or policy interpretation, or certify organizational compliance. Public repository guidance cannot represent nonpublic IC element implementation procedures.
