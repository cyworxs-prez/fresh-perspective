# Intelligence Summary

Periodic summary product (INTSUM-style). Apply your organization's markings, format, and release process — the repository never assigns markings. Trace every material item to a ledger or register ID; use the update-and-change-detection prompt (`prompts/functional/research-analysis/all-source/15-update-change-detection.md`) to distinguish new reality from new reporting before drafting.

```text
[ORGANIZATION MARKING BLOCK — assigned by the producing organization]

Title and reporting period:
Information cutoff:
Prepared by / approved by:
```

## Bottom line up front

The period's most decision-relevant development and its implication, with likelihood and confidence stated separately.

## Significant activity

One entry per material development, grouped by area, actor, or functional line as the audience expects:

| Item | Development (what happened) | Assessment (what it means) | Evidence (ledger ID) | Confidence | Change from prior period |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

Keep the development column to reported fact; keep inference and judgment in the assessment column.

## Changes from prior summary

- Judgments changed, with prior wording, new wording, and the diagnostic evidence:
- Judgments unchanged despite new reporting, and why:
- Corrections or retractions:

## Outlook

Expected developments in the next period, each with likelihood, the indicator that would confirm or deny it, and the decision it bears on.

## Gaps

Open requirements and the most consequential unknowns (requirement IDs).
